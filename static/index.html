Dicas rápidasQuick tips

Sensores a 0: troca TX com RX. Reinicia sozinha: alimenta por USB, pino 1 da X10A livre. Valores errados: Suggested Model → Instalar o firmware certo.

Sensors at 0: swap TX and RX. Keeps rebooting: power over USB, leave X10A pin 1 free. Wrong values: Suggested Model → Install the right firmware.
